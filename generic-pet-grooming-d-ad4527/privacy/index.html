What the request form collects

The request form asks for your pet's name, what your pet needs, your name, your phone number, and whether you would like a call or a text.

When you press Send request, your answers are sent by email through a form service called FormSubmit, so someone can call or text you back. They are not sold, and they are not used for anything else.
