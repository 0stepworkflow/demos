Privacy

Privacy

A plain-language note about the class request form on this site.

What the form collects

Your name, a phone number and/or email, the days and time window you prefer, what brings you in, how you would like to be contacted, and anything you choose to add in the notes box.

Where it goes

Your request is sent by email to 713 Boxing Club through a form-handling service (FormSubmit) so the club can follow up with you. It is used only to answer your request.

What we don't do

We don't sell your information, and we don't add you to marketing lists. This site has no advertising or analytics trackers and no embedded maps; "Get directions" opens Google Maps only when you tap it.

Questions or removal

To ask about or remove your information, call (832) 206-6837.
