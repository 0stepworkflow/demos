Privacy

Privacy

This is a plain-language note about the trial class request form on this site.

What the form collects

Your name, a phone number and/or email, the days and time window you prefer, the training direction you pick, how you would like to be contacted, and anything you choose to add in the notes box.

Where it goes

Your request is sent by email to 713 Boxing Club through a form-handling service (FormSubmit) so the club can follow up with you. It is used only to answer your request and confirm a time.

What we don’t do

We don’t sell your information, and we don’t add you to marketing lists. This site has no advertising or analytics trackers. The Location section embeds a Google Map, which is provided by Google.

Questions or removal

To ask about or remove your information, call (832) 206-6837.
